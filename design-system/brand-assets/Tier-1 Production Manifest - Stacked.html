Anti-patterns honoured

  • No drop shadows. Layering by whitespace.
  • No gradients. Solid Deep Ink on solid Paper Cream.
  • No rounded corners over 2 px.
  • No icons in chrome. Fish only. Care symbols are regulatory.
  • No exclamation marks. No marketing words.
  • Terracotta used exactly once — the wax seal.
