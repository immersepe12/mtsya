Anti-patterns honoured

  • No drop shadows. Layering is by whitespace and weight of paper.
  • No gradients. Solid Deep Ink on solid Paper Cream throughout.
  • No rounded corners over 2 px. Most pieces are square.
  • No icons in chrome. The fish is the only mark. Care symbols are regulatory.
  • No exclamation marks. No marketing words.
  • Terracotta used exactly once — the wax seal.
