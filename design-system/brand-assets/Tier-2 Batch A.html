Anti-patterns honoured

  • No drop shadows, no gradients, no rounded corners over 2 px.
  • No icons anywhere — language carries the meaning.
  • No exclamation marks, no “hurry,” no “limited.”
  • Terracotta makes no appearance in Batch A.
  • Backs are blank. The brand has nothing further to say there.

Awaiting review — before Batch B

  • Confirm dust-bag canvas weight (8 oz vs 10 oz).
  • Confirm letterhead margins (35 / 25 mm) before offset proof.
  • Confirm envelope ratio — both DL and C5, or DL only for season one.
  • Confirm the QC card’s presence in every shipment, or only the first 100.
  • Sign off on the postcard hero mood — Worn or Still for the first run.
