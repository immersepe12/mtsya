# The Story Rule (non-negotiable)

Every MTSYA design and asset must carry **at least a hint of the Matsya deluge story** —
the fish that carried Manu's boat through the flood. It need not be heavy, but a trace of
it must live in every piece.

**Why:** the myth is the brand's root. A design that could belong to any other brand is
off-brand no matter how good it looks. (Mirrors the quiet-luxury copy rule: "if it could
appear on any other brand unchanged, it is wrong.")

**How to apply:** before finalizing any prompt, asset or design, name the specific story
element it carries — waterline/tideline fade, the Scale, rising/receding water, the boat
(hull-plank seams), indigo-as-night-of-rain, "what survives," growth outgrowing vessels,
deluge drop codes, मत्स्य. If none is present, rework it. **Subtle is fine; absent is not.**
