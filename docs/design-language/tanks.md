# Tank-Top Design Language

Finalized July 2026. Sibling to [bottoms.md](bottoms.md) and
[streetwear-winning.md](streetwear-winning.md); the [story rule](story-rule.md) governs.

## Fit (locked — "fit is most important")
A **broad, straight, boxy sleeveless muscle tank** — the Nike athlete training-tank cut.
- WIDE squared shoulder-to-shoulder span; small **capped shoulder** with the seam sitting
  OUT at the natural shoulder point, fabric covering the deltoid.
- Armhole cut **straight down** in a vertical line (cut-off-sleeve look).
- From the armpits the body falls **dead straight** — vertical side seams, hem exactly as
  wide as the chest, NO taper, NO A-shape.
- Heavyweight cloth that holds the wide straight shape; ribbed crew neckline; straight
  boxy hem at the hip.
- NOT a deep-scooped gym tank, NOT a thin-strap vest, NOT slim-fit.

**On-model:** size it UP — capped shoulder extends slightly **past** the natural shoulder
point (a squared fabric shelf over the deltoid), deep straight-cut armhole, body wide and
roomy standing **away** from the torso, straight sides to a wide square boxy hem at the low
hip. Never clinging. Heavyweight cloth keeps it structured, not floppy. (Kajal was re-shot
broader to match Jamun when it read too closed.)

## Restraint
Tanks must be **calm**: one quiet hero texture, most of the surface plain, garment pressed
flat. First drafts were rejected as "too much happening / wrinkly."

## Sporty jersey only
Smooth athletic **jersey**, wash-led. Rib-knit "sweater-vest" tanks (Lehar) were rejected —
knit texture belongs on tees, not tanks.

## Logo placement lesson
Centre-chest reliably breaks the four-scale geometry (inflates to 6+ scales). Use the
**left chest**, on a calm patch, and guard the shape ("rounded scallop fish-scales, NOT
sharp geometric diamonds"). See [../logos/README.md](../logos/README.md).

## Marks
Four-scale Scale (thread one shade lighter, never white/bright) small on the left chest;
मत्स्य at the back nape; woven "DELUGE 01" side-hem tag.
