# Two Coexisting Design Directions

The repo holds **two deliberate brand directions** that share the Matsya/deluge myth but
otherwise contradict each other on purpose. Do **not** reconcile them.

## 1. Quiet luxury — `design-system/`
Plain shirts around ₹3,000, lowercase `mtsya`, the Madhubani/Matsya fish, Paper Cream
palette, no icons, "drop/limited" language banned. This governs the website and
e-commerce surfaces.

## 2. Streetwear — `research/streetwear-decode/`
Flood-washed heavyweight tees (₹1,900–2,499) + tanks + bottoms, the geometric **Scale**
emblem (four fish-scales), MTSYA caps in etched serif, deep indigo accent, numbered
no-restock drops (**DELUGE 01**), the Tide/flood-wash ghost-mark process.

**Why they contradict (intentionally):** scarcity vs anti-scarcity, caps vs lowercase,
wash-graphics vs no-graphics. Both were supplied knowingly. "Fixing" one to match the
other would destroy that direction.

**How to apply:**
- Streetwear garment mockups → follow the five-block prompt formula
  (see [generation.md](../generation.md) and `research/streetwear-decode/README.md`).
- Website / e-commerce → follow `design-system/`.
- Never use the Madhubani fish in streetwear assets or the Scale in quiet-luxury surfaces
  (see [../logos/README.md](../logos/README.md)).
