# MTSYA — DELUGE 01 Oversized Tee · Measurement Specification (POM Sheet)

**Garment:** oversized heavyweight t-shirt — one master block for all six tees
(Depth, Net, Ripple, Abyss, Current, Rekha).
**Fabric:** heavyweight cotton jersey / speciality knits, 260–300 GSM, garment-washed.
**Fit intent (anchored to the finalized on-model images, benchmark = Current on Karan):**
boxy and oversized; shoulder seam dropped ~5–6 cm past the natural shoulder point onto the
upper bicep; short, wide sleeves ending mid-bicep (~10–12 cm above the elbow crease on a
183 cm wearer) with the opening standing off the arm; body wide, standing away from the
torso; **dead-straight side seams** — hem sweep ≈ chest; hem at the low hip, just covering
the trouser waistband; substantial ribbed crew collar sitting close to the neck.

**All measurements are FINISHED, POST-WASH, in centimetres.**
Width-type points are measured **flat (half)** — garment laid flat, measured straight
across one layer — the standard tech-pack convention.

## 2 · Two sleeve constructions, one silhouette

| Styles | Construction | Controlling POMs |
|---|---|---|
| **Abyss, Net, Rekha** | Drop-shoulder **set-in** sleeve | E (across shoulder), F, G, I + all body POMs |
| **Current, Depth, Ripple** | **Raglan** sleeve (seam runs collar → underarm) | **J (CB neck → sleeve hem)** replaces E+I; G and H unchanged; raglan seam length ≈ 31 / 32 / 33 / 34 (ref) |

Both must deliver the identical worn silhouette: sleeve hem landing mid-bicep, opening
standing off the arm, same chest/hem/length. Grade J at +2.0 per size in both cases.

## 3 · Pattern & making notes (for the patternmaker)

- **Shrinkage — critical:** all values above are FINISHED AFTER garment/flood wash.
  Cut patterns larger by the tested shrinkage of each fabric. Provisional allowance until
  wash trials: **+5 % length, +3 % width** for 260–300 GSM cotton jersey; heavy stone/salt
  washes can pull more — run a 50 cm wash-test square per fabric and set exact factors.
  Rib collar shrinks more than body: cut rib with +6–8 % length allowance.
- **Seam allowances:** 1.0 cm shoulder / armhole / side seams / raglan seams · 0.7 cm
  collar attach · 2.5 cm turned twin-needle hems (body + sleeve).
- **Construction:** overlocked seams throughout; twin-needle coverstitch on hems;
  back-neck tape HPS-to-HPS; shoulder seams taped or clean-finished (set-in styles).
  1×1 rib collar, same dye-lot, finished height 2.2 cm.
- **Per-style finish exceptions:** **Abyss** and **Depth** carry the raw-cut hem detail —
  bottom (and Abyss sleeve) hems left raw to curl after wash: hem depths P/Q = 0 for those
  edges; add 1 cm to cut length to compensate for curl. **Net** is an open-gauge knit and
  **Rekha** a fine-gauge knit — same finished POMs; knit panels to gauge, expect higher
  relaxation, wash-test mandatory. Tonal hull-seam / panel details per each style's
  flat-lay; panels are internal to these outer dimensions and do not alter any POM.
- **Fabric consumption (est.):** ~1.35 m per tee at 180 cm tubular/open width, size M.
- **Base size for sampling: M.** Fit-check on a 100–104 cm chest, ~180 cm wearer against
  the benchmark image (Current on Karan): shoulder seam ~5–6 cm past shoulder point,
  sleeve hem ~10–12 cm above elbow crease, hem at low hip covering waistband, body
  standing visibly away from torso at chest and hem.
