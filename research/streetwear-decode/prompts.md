## Prompt 1 — MTSYA "Tide Tee" (drop hero t-shirt)

> Create a photorealistic apparel mockup of a premium oversized streetwear t-shirt, front and back view, flat-lay on a neutral concrete-grey background.
>
> Garment: boxy oversized fit, drop shoulders, heavyweight 260 GSM cotton jersey with visible fabric texture, ribbed collar. Color: "Monsoon" — a washed grey-blue with an acid/salt-wash finish, uneven mottled fading like it survived a flood. The wash must look organic and irregular, not printed.
>
> Front: small tonal fish-scale emblem on the left chest — a single geometric scale shape made of two overlapping crescents, revealed lighter through the wash (a ghost mark, tone-on-tone, "earned not printed").
> Back: large hero graphic — a tessellating pattern of the same geometric fish scales rising from the hem and dissolving/fading toward the shoulders like a receding waterline, in deep indigo tone-on-tone. Above it, the wordmark "MTSYA" in an engraved etched-serif typeface. Small Devanagari text "मत्स्य" at the back nape.
>
> Style: premium Indian streetwear, in the lane of Roxylane and Almost Gods — mythology through process, not clip-art. No fish illustrations, no god imagery, no loud colors. Moody, editorial product-photography lighting. Include a small woven indigo hem label reading "DELUGE 01".

## Prompt 2 — MTSYA "Ghat Utility Pant" (knit-utility hero pant)

> Create a photorealistic apparel mockup of premium streetwear utility pants, front and three-quarter back view, flat-lay on a neutral concrete-grey background.
>
> Garment: oversized straight-leg fit, cut from heavyweight 380 GSM French terry knit (visible loopback texture), garment-washed in "Abyss" — washed black with a subtle blue-indigo cast, uneven acid-wash fading concentrated at the knees and hem like a waterline.
>
> Construction: 8 pockets total — two 3D pentagon-shaped cargo pockets on the thighs with snap flaps, angled front pockets, layered back pockets. Elastic waistband with heavy drawcord. Exposed contrast stitching in a warm sand "Silt" tone. Tonal embroidered fish-scale emblem (two overlapping geometric crescents) on the lower left calf. Small woven deep-indigo label on the back pocket reading "MTSYA — DELUGE 01".
>
> Style: knit comfort with technical-utility attitude — premium Indian streetwear between a cargo pant and a sweatpant, in the lane of Roxylane's utility line. Moody editorial lighting, fabric texture clearly visible, no models, no props.
